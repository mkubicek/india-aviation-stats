# Noida thesis register

The Noida page (`noida.html`) is built as a set of theses, each tied to one
exhibit and recomputed from the published DGCA tables on every refresh. This
file is the durable index of those theses; the numbers themselves live on the
generated page and are never copied here, so they cannot go stale.

## How the register works

- Each exhibit on the page states one thesis in its computed title.
- The page's **"Claims tested against the data"** section lists
  counter-narratives checked against the same tables. Every verdict sentence
  is generated by `scripts/noida.py` (`register_entries()`) in the same run as
  the exhibits, and the verdict WORD is derived from the computed signs, so a
  refresh that flips a number flips the verdict with it. Verdicts state what
  the data shows; they never assert causes.
- Claims that cannot be tested from currently published tables render under
  **"Not yet testable"**, with the input they wait on. They are not silently
  omitted, and the untestable entry is dropped the moment its test goes live.
- A skipped exhibit drops its register entry rather than showing a stale
  number, and renders as a visible "pending" placeholder on the page.

## Theses (one per exhibit)

| Thesis | Exhibit | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| National growth is paused against its long-run trend | `noida_growth_pause` | passengers carried (carrier table) |

| Two airline groups carry most domestic passengers | `noida_airline_groups` | passengers-carried share |

| Incumbent pace vs the rest of the country | `noida_del_vs_rest` | airport passenger movements, indexed |
| Delhi is the international gateway benchmark | `noida_del_international` | Indian gateway throughput share |
| An NCR year has a repeatable seasonal shape | `noida_del_seasonality` | airport throughput index |
| UP's own airports already handle a measurable regional market | `noida_up_catchment` | airport passenger movements |
| Delhi's observable route markets are the route-development research queue | `noida_delhi_menu` | route-segment passengers |

Metric provenance per exhibit is machine-readable in
`charts/noida/manifest.json` (`primary_source_table`, `metric_semantics`).

## Claims currently in the tested register

- "Airlines have stopped adding domestic capacity."
- "The domestic market is fragmenting across many carriers."
- "Noida opens into empty white space no airline serves."

## White space

"Noida opens into empty white space no airline serves." is now computed on
every refresh from `data/processed/domestic_route_monthly.csv`: candidates
are domestic airports above a disclosed annual route-movement floor outside
the NCR set, and the verdict counts those absent from Delhi's observed menu.
The grading (rejected / largely rejected / partly supported) derives from
the computed ratio.

## Provenance note

The original exploration that narrowed a wider candidate list to these theses
predates this register and was not committed to the repository. This register
is the recomputable subset: only claims whose tests run against committed,
published tables appear here, so every listed verdict can be re-derived by
running `scripts/noida.py`.
