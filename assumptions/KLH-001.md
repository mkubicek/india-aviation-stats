# Observation

The first cut of `mappings.yaml` filed Kalaburagi/Gulbarga airport under the key
`KLH`. But `KLH` is **Kolhapur's** IATA code (Maharashtra); Kalaburagi/Gulbarga
(Karnataka) is **GBI**. The raw data carries `KALABURAGI KARNATAKA`
(2019-2022), `KALABURAGI` (2022-2025), and `GULBARGA` (2026-06), plus a separate
`KOLHAPUR` series (2018-2026) — two physically distinct airports ~500 km apart.

# Interpretation

Mapping the unmapped `KOLHAPUR` label to `KLH` while Kalaburagi's data already sat
under `KLH` would have merged two different airports into one series — a silent
erase. This was caught during the airport-label verification pass.

# Decision

Kalaburagi moved to **GBI** (variants `KALABURAGI`, `KALABURAGI KARNATAKA`,
`GULBARGA`); a fresh **KLH** rebuilt for Kolhapur (variant `KOLHAPUR`). Distinct
entities, never merged.

# Evidence

- Airports Authority of India: Kalaburagi Airport is IATA **GBI**, located east
  of Gulbarga — https://www.aai.aero/en/airports/kalaburagi
- AAI eAIP: VOGB is Kalaburagi Airport —
  https://aim-india.aai.aero/eaip/eaip-v2-04-2026/eAIP/IN-AD%202.1VOGB-en-GB.html
- Kolhapur Airport (Chhatrapati Rajaram Maharaj) = KLH / VAKP —
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolhapur_Airport
