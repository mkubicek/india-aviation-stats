# Observation

The June 2026 domestic city-pair workbook introduces `GAUTAM BUDDHA NAGAR` as
an endpoint in six city-pair markets. It first appears in the same month
that Noida International Airport began commercial passenger operations.

# Interpretation

The source label is the district containing Noida International Airport, not a
second airport. The airport's official IATA code is DXN, its concession site is
in Gautam Buddha Nagar district, and its official opening notice states that
commercial operations began on 15 June 2026.

# Decision

`GAUTAM BUDDHA NAGAR` (from 2026-06) resolves to canonical **DXN**. The first
observation is a partial opening month and must not be interpreted as a
steady-state demand estimate.
