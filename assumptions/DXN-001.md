# Observation

A new domestic city-pair label `GAUTAM BUDDHA NAGAR` first appears in the
June 2026 DGCA workbook: 20,058 passengers across six pairs (Amritsar,
Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Jammu, Lucknow, Navi Mumbai), with no history in any
earlier month. Gautam Buddh Nagar is the Uttar Pradesh district containing
Jewar, the site of Noida International Airport (DXN), which opened in 2026.
DGCA labels several 2026 entries by administrative region rather than city
(the same batch introduced other long-form labels).

# Interpretation

The label is the opening month of DXN, published under its district name.
The pattern matches a partial opening month (small volume, few pairs), and
matches the airport's public opening timeline. It is NOT a relabel of an
existing NCR airport: DEL and HDO both continue under their own labels in
the same month.

# Decision

`GAUTAM BUDDHA NAGAR` (valid from 2026-06) → **DXN**.

# Evidence

- Noida International Airport, Jewar, Gautam Buddh Nagar district -
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noida_International_Airport
- Commercial flight operations began 15 June 2026 (aerodrome licence 6 March
  2026; Phase 1 inaugurated 28 March 2026), so the June DGCA row is a half-month
  by construction - https://newsonair.gov.in/noida-international-airport-to-begin-commercial-flight-operations-from-15-june-2026/
- June 2026 DGCA city-pair workbook: first appearance, 20,058 pax, 6 pairs.
- DEL and HDO rows continue independently in June 2026.

# Falsification

`distinct-airports-not-merged` (airports=[DEL, HDO, DXN]): re-open if any of
the three NCR airports disappears from the domestic monthly table, which
would suggest the label was a relabel or merge of an existing airport rather
than a new one.
