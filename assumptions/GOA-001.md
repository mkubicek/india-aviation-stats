# Observation

Goa has two airports: **Dabolim** (IATA GOI, South Goa, the older/larger airport)
and **Manohar International / Mopa** (IATA GOX, North Goa, opened 2023-01-05). DGCA's
domestic labels are paradoxical — the source files Mopa's traffic under *Dabolim's*
own IATA code. By magnitude, the larger `DABOLIM` series is physical Dabolim and the
smaller `GOI` series (present 2015-2018, then again 2023+) is physical **Mopa** from
2023. The bare label `GOI` therefore means Dabolim through 2018 (the only airport
then) and Mopa from 2023.

Raw provenance (domestic city aggregate):
- `GOA` 2017-06 → Dabolim era (only airport)
- `GOA` 2024-06 → Mopa era (≈4.6M/yr airport)
- `DABOLIM` 2019+ → physical Dabolim

# Interpretation

A flat alias would be wrong by construction, and the obvious merge `DABOLIM`+`GOI`
would erase Mopa. The mapping is temporal and must use validity windows. `GOA`
(≤2018-12), `DABOLIM`, `GOA DABOLIM SOUTH GOA` → **GOI** (Dabolim). `GOA`
(≥2023-01), `MOPA GOA`, `GOA MOPA NORTH GOA` → **GOX** (Mopa). Never sum DABOLIM+GOI.

# Decision

Two distinct canonical entities with windowed variants in `mappings.yaml`. The
resolver refuses to build if any label maps to both with overlapping windows.
