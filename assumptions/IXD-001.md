# Observation

Prayagraj Airport (Bamrauli, IATA IXD) appears under three source labels as the city
was renamed Allahabad → Prayagraj (2018) and DGCA later adopted a long form:
`ALLAHABAD`, `PRAYAGRAJ`, and `ALLAHABAD PRAYAGRAJ` (2026 long-form). Same physical
airport throughout.

# Interpretation

This is the easy class, for contrast with Goa: a contiguous rename of one airport,
not two airports hiding behind one name. Safe to merge once a human confirms the
city rename.

# Decision

All three labels → **IXD**. No validity windows needed beyond the long-form
appearing in 2026.

# Evidence

- Prayagraj Airport (formerly Allahabad) = IXD —
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prayagraj_Airport

# Falsification

`month-disjoint-rename` (labels=[ALLAHABAD, PRAYAGRAJ, ALLAHABAD PRAYAGRAJ]):
re-open if any two of these labels carry traffic in the *same* month (which would
mean they are not a clean rename of a single airport).
