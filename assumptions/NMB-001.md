# Observation

A new domestic city-pair label `DAMAN` first appears in the July 2026 DGCA
workbook: one pair (Delhi), 166 passengers (86 to Delhi, 80 from Delhi), with
no history in any earlier month. Left unmapped, the label also broke the
per-month `sum(departures) == sum(arrivals)` conservation tripwire by exactly
6 passengers, because the Delhi endpoint was attributed and the Daman endpoint
was not.

# Interpretation

Daman Airport (ICAO `VADN`, IATA `NMB`) is the Coast Guard airstrip at Nani
Daman in the union territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu. A
civil terminal built alongside it, branded NAMO Airport, was inaugurated on
5 June 2026, and Alliance Air's Daman-Delhi service was flagged off on
16 July 2026, operating every Monday and Wednesday. That is the first scheduled
commercial passenger service the airport has had, which is why the label has no
history before 2026-07.

The volume is consistent with a part-month at that frequency: at most five
departures fall in July after the 16th, so 86 outbound passengers is about 17
per flight on an Alliance Air ATR (48 or 70 seats).

It is NOT a relabel of Diu (`DIU`), the other civil airport in the same union
territory: DIU continues under its own label in the same month (10,874
passengers across 10 rows in July 2026), and Daman and Diu are ~650 km apart by
road.

# Decision

`DAMAN` -> **NMB** (resolved through the entry's implicit `city` label; no
variants declared, so `DAMAN` and `DAMAN AIRPORT` both resolve and any other
DGCA spelling surfaces as an advisory rather than resolving silently).

The IATA code `NMB` is not published in the eAIP (which carries ICAO codes only)
and is corroborated by commercial booking systems routing Delhi-Daman as
`DEL-NMB`; no competing assignment of `NMB` was found. Reporting that regular
service began on 20 July 2026 appears only in trade press and is not relied on
here: the DGCA row covers the whole month either way.
