## Methodology

Projections use a log-log OLS regression of flights per capita on GDP per capita (PPP), fitted on 20 years of Indian data excluding COVID years (2020–2021). GDP for 2025–2040 uses log-linear extrapolation from the last 10 observed years; IMF WEO integration is a planned v1.1 upgrade.
